# T502 — MC normalization and independent uncertainty propagation

A simulated sample has cross section 2 pb, sum of generated event weights 100000, selected sum of weights 4000, and selected sum of squared weights 6000. Target integrated luminosity is 10 fb^-1. Normalize selected yield by L*sigma/sumw_generated. Treat the generated normalization denominator as fixed, and selected MC variance by sumw2. Independent relative luminosity and cross-section uncertainties are 2% and 10%. Compute expected yield, the three uncertainty components, and their quadrature total. Do not use the raw event count in place of the generated sum of weights.

Return a concise answer, explicit assumptions, checkable calculations or evidence, requested code/artifacts, and confidence from 0 to 1 that all critical criteria are satisfied. Do not supply a private reasoning transcript.

Critical requirements:
- Correct normalization and units
- Correct uncertainty propagation
