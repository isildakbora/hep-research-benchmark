# T501 — Poisson discovery statistic with known background

For a single Poisson count n=30 and exactly known background b=20, signal yield s is constrained to s>=0. No nuisance parameters are present. Compute the MLE of s and the one-sided discovery statistic q0=-2 log[L(s=0)/L(s_hat)]. Under the stated asymptotic convention report local Z=sqrt(q0). Explain why s/sqrt(b) is not the exact likelihood-ratio expression, and why this is not a global significance or a result with background uncertainty.

Return a concise answer, explicit assumptions, checkable calculations or evidence, requested code/artifacts, and confidence from 0 to 1 that all critical criteria are satisfied. Do not supply a private reasoning transcript.

Critical requirements:
- Correct constrained MLE and likelihood ratio
- Correct significance and assumptions
