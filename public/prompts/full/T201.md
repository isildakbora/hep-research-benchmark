# T201 — Invariant mass and common longitudinal boost

Two massless particles have (pT,eta,phi)=(40 GeV,0,0) and (40 GeV,0,pi). Compute their pair invariant mass and pair transverse momentum. Then apply a common longitudinal boost that adds +1 to both particle rapidities (equal to eta here). Give the new pair energy and longitudinal momentum and verify the invariant mass. Use c=1 and state the formulas.

Return a concise answer, explicit assumptions, checkable calculations or evidence, requested code/artifacts, and confidence from 0 to 1 that all critical criteria are satisfied. Do not supply a private reasoning transcript.

Critical requirements:
- Correct invariant mass and transverse momentum
- Correct boosted four-vector and invariance
